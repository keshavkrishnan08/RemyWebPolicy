Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2, 2025

Last Updated: October 2, 2025

Remy ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the limited data we gather when you use Remy (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

When you use Remy, we only collect the following information:

  • Username – chosen by you for identification within the app.
  • Email Address – used for account registration, communication, and account recovery.
  • AI Usage Data – including text queries, interactions with the AI, and feature usage within the app.

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as financial data, health data, government identifiers, or location data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To Provide the Service – Username and email are used to create and manage your account. AI usage data is used to process your requests and deliver responses.
  • To Improve the App – Aggregated and anonymized AI usage data may be analyzed to improve performance, reliability, and features.
  • To Communicate With You – We may use your email address to send service-related updates, respond to inquiries, and provide account support.
  • To Ensure Security – Basic logging of AI usage helps detect abuse, prevent fraud, and ensure fair usage of the app.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share limited information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted vendors who help us operate the app (e.g., cloud hosting, authentication, analytics).
  • Legal Requirements: If required to comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or enforceable government requests.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the new entity.

4. Data Retention

  • Username and Email: Retained for as long as your account remains active.
  • AI Usage Data: Retained temporarily for system performance and abuse detection. Some anonymized data may be stored longer for analytics purposes.

You may request deletion of your account and associated information at any time (see Section 7 below).

5. Security of Information

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Remy is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us to request deletion.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the following rights:

  • Access – You may request a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Correction – You may request that we update or correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion – You may request deletion of your account and associated information.
  • Withdrawal of Consent – You may stop using the app at any time and request removal of your data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at keshav-krishnan@outlook.com.

8. International Users

If you access Remy from outside the United States, note that your information may be transferred to and processed in countries where data protection laws may differ from your home country.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted within the app and take effect on the "Effective Date" shown at the top of the policy.
